- An action that has not answered within 60 s (merge, update, a status change, rerun) is stopped too, so later actions of the same kind are no longer stuck behind it. Its card says what may already have happened instead of calling it failed, for example `! may be merged`, the footer adds `no answer in 60s`, the details pane (`d`) tells when conveyor stopped waiting, and conveyor reloads the pull requests as it would after a success. After a merge, an add to the merge queue or enabling auto-merge that timed out, updating the branch and converting to draft wait until My PRs has caught up, as after one that answered.
